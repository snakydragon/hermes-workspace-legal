Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 October 2026

Hermes Workspace is a personal tool used only by its owner, Grimaldy Sinaga. It is not a public service.

What it accesses

With the owner's explicit consent, the app accesses the owner's own Google account data (such as Google Sheets, Drive, Gmail, Calendar and Docs) to perform tasks the owner requests.

How data is handled

  • Data is processed only on the owner's own computer.
  • Data is not sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.
  • Access tokens are stored locally on the owner's computer and nowhere else.
  • The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access

The owner can revoke access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
